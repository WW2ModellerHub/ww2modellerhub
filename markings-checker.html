About this checker

The checker lists the camouflage, national markings and identification markings that were standard for an air force, theatre and date. It covers the changes that most often catch modellers out: the RAF underside changes of 1940, the 1941 Day Fighter Scheme, the 1942 roundel change, US national star changes, invasion stripes, and the main Luftwaffe, Japanese, Soviet and Italian scheme changes.

Orders took time to reach every unit, and photos of real aircraft always beat general rules. Use the result as a checklist, then confirm against a dated photo of your subject. For colours, use the paint converter.

Quick answers

When did the RAF change from Dark Earth to Ocean Grey?

The Day Fighter Scheme of Ocean Grey and Dark Green over Medium Sea Grey was introduced in August 1941.

When did US aircraft lose the red centre in the star?

In May 1942, to avoid confusion with the Japanese hinomaru.

When were invasion stripes used?

From D-Day, 6 June 1944, on Allied tactical aircraft. They were removed from upper surfaces from late summer 1944.

Is the checker always right for my aircraft?

It gives the typical rules for a date. Individual aircraft varied, especially near a changeover, so always check a photo of the aircraft you are modelling.
