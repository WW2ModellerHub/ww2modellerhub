Petlyakov Pe-2

Pe-2 / Pe-2FT · Soviet VVS · 1941–1945
Allied desert, Pacific and Flying Tigers fighter

The Petlyakov Pe-2 was the Soviet Union’s fast twin-engine bomber and dive-bomber workhorse: sleek, versatile and used from the desperate defensive battles of 1941 through Kursk, Bagration and Berlin. For modellers it means a glazed nose, twin nacelles, defensive gun stations, bomb-bay and external load choices, VVS camouflage, red stars, bort numbers, nacelle exhaust staining and rough-field bomber weathering.

Pe-2 operations · 1941–1945

The Pe-2 was one of the most important Soviet twin-engine aircraft of the war. It served as a fast bomber, dive bomber, reconnaissance aircraft and attack platform, appearing across Moscow, Stalingrad, Kursk, Bagration and the final offensive into Germany. For a model, the challenge is bomber detail rather than fighter simplicity: cockpit and nose glazing, crew stations, nacelle seams, defensive guns, bomb racks, underside grime and red-star/bort-number placement.

Variant warning

Pe-2, Pe-2FT and reconnaissance aircraft differ in guns, glazing, equipment and markings. Check the exact aircraft before decals.

Glazing warning

The Pe-2 is a clear-parts project. Mask the nose/cockpit early and plan interior painting before closing the fuselage.

Modeller shortcuts

Build checklist

  • Pick exact Pe-2A/B/night fighter variant, BAP/Guards BAP/recon unit, front, markings and date
  • Check Red Star / BAP markings, group colours, squadron codes and date
  • Treat early AMT/AII, desert, winter, Reich defence and late-war mottling differently
  • Add Bramo exhaust, nacelle oil, gun staining, dust/mud and narrow-gear wear
  • Check Pe-2 glazing, guns, fighter stores, gun bay, camera/recon fit, armoured attackes, stores and markings before paint
