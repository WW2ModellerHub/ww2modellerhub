Quick answers

What RAF colours do I need for a Battle of Britain aircraft?

Common colours are RAF Dark Green, Dark Earth, Sky Type S, Interior Grey-Green, Night/black and aluminium for chipping or details.

Are paint brand matches exact?

They are practical modelling equivalents. Always check the aircraft date, theatre and finish before treating any brand match as exact.
