Battle of Britain Route

A modelling story of RAF fighter defence, Luftwaffe raids, patched airframes, quick turnarounds and early-war camouflage decisions.

Why this route matters

The Battle of Britain route is ideal for modellers because the aircraft, markings and weathering are tightly linked to real airfields. Use the map to decide whether your build is a front-line Hurricane, a Spitfire squadron aircraft, or a Bf 109 operating across the Channel.
