USAAF heavy bomber campaign · 1941–1945

The B-24 Liberator was the defining USAAF heavy bomber of the daylight strategic bombing campaign against Germany, while also flying significant operations from the Mediterranean and the early-war Pacific. 8th Air Force Liberator groups from East Anglia bore the brunt of the European campaign; 15th Air Force groups attacked southern Europe from Italy; and the 19th and 43rd Bomb Groups fought the early Pacific war from the Philippines, Australia and New Guinea. Choosing your B-24 kit

For 1:48 the HK Models B-24D or B-24J is the centrepiece option for serious modelling. For 1:72 the Airfix B-24J (A08017B) is the safest mainstream recommendation, with Revell or Academy B-24D for Strawberry Bitch subjects. For Pacific or Mediterranean F variants, check chin turret, waist windows and tail position before you start.

Aftermarket for the B-24 Liberator

The Liberator benefits enormously from aftermarket — Eduard SPACE 3D for the cockpit, Master Model brass barrels for all 13 .50 cal positions, weighted resin wheels, and good masks for the huge expanse of glazing. Decals matter as much as detail — pick the exact aircraft and date before buying anything.

USAAF B-24 paint colours

Two main looks dominate B-24 modelling: early/mid-war Olive Drab 41 over Neutral Gray 43 on B-24D subjects, and the classic late-war natural-metal finish on B-24J aircraft (with an Olive Drab anti-glare panel). Tail symbols, cowl rings and group identity colours do most of the visual work on the finished model.

National insignia & marking notes

Pre-mid-1942: Roundel-style red dot in centre of white star, no bars.

Mid-1942 → mid-1943: Star and bar without red surround. The familiar mid-war marking.

Jun–Sep 1943: Brief red-bar variant — important for some 91st BG and other early-war modelling subjects.

Sep 1943 onwards: Standard blue-surround star and bar carried for the rest of the war.

Tail symbols: Triangle (1st BD), Square (3rd BD), Circle (15th AF). Letter inside identifies the bomb group — Triangle A = 91st, Square D = 100th, Triangle K = 379th, etc.
