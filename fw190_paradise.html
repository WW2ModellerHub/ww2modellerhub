Focke-Wulf Fw 190

Fw 190A / F / G / D-9 · Luftwaffe · 1941–1945
Luftwaffe route with Gruppe, Staffel and theatre markings context

The Focke-Wulf Fw 190 was the Luftwaffe’s rugged radial-engined fighter and fighter-bomber: compact, powerful, heavily armed and feared over the Channel Front, Eastern Front and Germany. For modellers it offers brutal A-series fighters, Jabo fighter-bombers, F/G ground-attack aircraft, late-war Dora long-nose variants, Reich defence bands, mottling, winter whitewash and dirty operational finishes.

Fw 190 operations · 1941–1945

The Fw 190 gave the Luftwaffe a powerful, heavily armed radial fighter that shocked the RAF when it appeared over the Channel Front. It later became a workhorse fighter-bomber and ground-attack aircraft across the Eastern Front, before late-war long-nose Doras fought in the Defence of the Reich. For modellers, it is one of the best Luftwaffe subjects: broad wheel stance, heavy cowling, wing-root guns, mottled greys, yellow theatre bands, winter whitewash and late-war RLM 81/82/76 complexity.

Variant warning

Fw 190A, F, G and D variants are not interchangeable. Check cowling, wing armament, racks, canopy, tail and engine length before building.

Loadout warning

Fw 190s carried tanks, bombs, rockets and gun packs, but not randomly. Match weapons fit to unit, front and date.

Modeller shortcuts

Build checklist

  • Pick exact Anton/F/G/Dora variant, JG/SG unit, front, markings and date
  • Check Balkenkreuz / JG & SG markings, group colours, squadron codes and date
  • Treat early RLM, desert, winter, Reich defence and late-war mottling differently
  • Add BMW exhaust, cowling oil, gun staining, dust/mud and narrow-gear wear
  • Check Anton/F/G/Dora cowling, canopy, racks, wheels, stores and markings before paint
