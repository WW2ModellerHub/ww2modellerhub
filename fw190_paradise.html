Aftermarket for the Fw 190

The Airfix kit cockpit benefits hugely from the CMK CMK4352 resin set — it's the single best upgrade for the money. For gun barrels, the Master Model AM-48-172 brass set makes an immediate visible difference. Eduard's pre-cut canopy masks save time and improve results over freehand. Photo-etch is optional on the Airfix kit but nearly mandatory on the older Hasegawa and Eduard plastics. Battle of Britain paint colours

The Fw 190 wore the RLM system throughout its operational life: RLM 74 Graugrün and RLM 75 Grauviolett on upper surfaces, RLM 76 Lichtblau on undersurfaces and as fuselage mottling base. Cockpit in RLM 66 Schwarzgrau. Eastern Front aircraft add RLM 04 Gelb markings. Lighten upper surfaces 10–15% for 1:48, 15–20% for 1:72.

Luftwaffe marking notes

July–Aug 1940: Type A1 roundels (red/white/blue, no yellow ring) on fuselage. Type A upper wings. Type B lower wings (red/blue only). Large fin flash.

Sep–Oct 1940: Yellow outer ring on fuselage roundels increasingly common. Yellow border on upper wing roundels appearing late in period.

Code letters: Sky BS381C-216. Approx 24 inch height. Serial in black on rear fuselage port side only.

Cockpit
Modeller's Note Interior is RLM 66 Schwarzgrau (Tamiya XF-63, Vallejo 70.862) from 1941 onwards — a very dark near-black grey. Earlier aircraft used RLM 02 Grau. Instrument panel in RLM 66 with instrument faces in flat black. Sight reflector glass in pale green. The Revi gunsight is RLM 66. Seat cushion in dark leather. Both the RAF Museum Cosford A-8 and Hendon A-8/R11 have accessible cockpits for reference photography.

Exhaust Stacks & Cowling
Modeller's Note The BMW 801 has 14 individual exhaust stacks arranged in two banks of 7 around the cowling ring. They produce a distinctive star-burst staining pattern — dark brown-black streaks radiating rearward from each stack. The lower cowling shows less staining than the upper. The A-7 and A-8 have the distinctive paired MG 131 bulges on the upper cowling immediately behind the spinner — a key identification feature.

Undercarriage
Modeller's Note Undercarriage legs are RLM 02 Grau on early aircraft, RLM 66 on later production. Wheel bay interiors also RLM 02 or 66. The main gear has an inward-retracting geometry unique to the Fw 190. Wheels in RLM 02 with warm mid-grey tyres. The tailwheel is fixed on early variants, semi-retractable on later A-series.
Markings reference
Modeller's Note Balkenkreuz on upper and lower wings and fuselage sides. Swastika on fin. Individual aircraft letter in white (or yellow on Eastern Front aircraft). Gruppe bar after the letter — none for I. Gruppe, one bar for II., two for III. Staffel colour shown on spinner ring. On Eastern Front aircraft, yellow RLM 04 fuselage band and cowling tip are standard.
