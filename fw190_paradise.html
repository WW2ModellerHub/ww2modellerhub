Luftwaffe reference

Focke-Wulf Fw 190.

Kurt Tank's radial-engined answer to the Spitfire — the Focke-Wulf Fw 190 gave the Luftwaffe air superiority over the Channel Front from 1941. For modellers it means BMW 801 cowling detail, varied camouflage schemes and a range of excellent kits.

Modeller quick start

The Fw 190 A-8 is the most kitted variant and the natural starting point. Eduard's 1:48 ProfiPACK remains the benchmark for detail and fit. The Dora is best served by Hasegawa's long-running but still-competitive 1:48 tooling.
