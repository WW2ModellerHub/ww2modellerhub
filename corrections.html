Corrections and Contributions

How modellers can suggest corrections, new references, better kit recommendations, image fixes, paint notes and historical improvements for WW2 Modeller.

What to send

Useful corrections include: wrong aircraft images, incorrect paint references, broken links, better kit recommendations, missing aftermarket, wrong squadron details, better book recommendations, or additional theatre/history context.

Please include the page URL, the issue, the suggested correction and, where possible, the source or reference that supports it.

Contribution ideas

Build photos, walkaround links, kit notes, paint testing notes, book recommendations, specific squadron markings, correction notes and useful YouTube technique videos are all valuable additions.

Contact route

Use the Contact page for now, or include correction details when sharing the site on modelling forums. A dedicated form may be added later.
