The core colours of every major WW2 air force on one page, with the closest common hobby paints. For the full searchable list, use the paint codes page.

How to use this chart

Find the air force and colour, then pick the brand you already use. Mixing brands is fine, but stick to one paint type (acrylic or lacquer) per layer to avoid compatibility problems. Equivalents are close matches, not perfect ones — wartime paints varied and faded, so a slightly lighter or weathered shade is often more convincing on a model.

Paint matches are approximate. Wartime paint varied by batch and weathered quickly, and photos change colour. Use these as a starting point and see the paint disclaimer.

Quick answers

Can I mix Tamiya and Vallejo paints?

Yes, between layers once each is dry. Avoid mixing them together in the airbrush cup.

Are these exact matches?

No. They are close equivalents; real wartime colours varied and faded.
