Quick answers

What is the best first WW2 aircraft model kit?

A Hurricane, Spitfire, Bf 109, P-51 or Hellcat is usually a better first subject than a large bomber because the kit is smaller, cheaper and easier to finish well.

Should I buy aftermarket for every kit?

No. Start with canopy masks, seatbelts and decals where visible. Avoid hidden resin unless the model will be displayed open.
