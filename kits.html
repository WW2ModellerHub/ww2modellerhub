Favourite kit decision guide

1

Pick scale first

1/72 for collections, 1/48 for balanced detail, 1/32 for statement builds. Do not buy aftermarket before scale and subject are fixed.

2

Choose the exact airframe

Codes, serial, theatre and date decide the propeller, canopy, aerials, bombs, tanks and paint route.

3

Buy visible upgrades only

Seatbelts, wheels, exhausts, gun barrels and decals normally show. Hidden resin rarely earns its keep.

4

Match decals to paint

Decals should drive your camouflage scheme, not the other way round. Check theatre markings before committing.

Aftermarket shortcuts

Priority 1

Seatbelts and cockpit placards

Best value on open-canopy fighters and large-scale cockpits.
