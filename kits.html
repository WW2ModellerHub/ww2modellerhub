Kit buying guide

Starter kitsSpitfire Mk I, Hurricane Mk I, Bf 109E, P-51D

Prioritise fit, clear instructions and simple camouflage.

Detail buildsBf 109G, Fw 190A, Corsair, Mosquito, Lancaster

Choose kits with cockpit, wheel well and surface detail potential.

Weathering buildsHurricane, Il-2, Zero, P-40, Ju 87

Good subjects for dust, chipping, exhaust and field repairs.

Aftermarket prioritiesSeatbelts, masks, wheels, exhausts, decals

Buy upgrades that remain visible after the canopy/fuselage is closed.

Decal routesSquadron-specific sheets and stencil sets

Best value when building real pilots, squadrons and theatres.

Avoid firstVery old tooling, warped short-run kits, unclear transparencies

Only tackle these when you want a restoration challenge.
