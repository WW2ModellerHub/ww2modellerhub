Best starter route

Choose a modern 1/72 or 1/48 kit with simple camouflage, limited glazing and strong fit reviews. Avoid heavy photo-etch until you know the base kit goes together cleanly.

  • Spitfire, Hurricane, P-51, Bf 109 and Zero are good first aircraft subjects.
  • Large bombers need more space, more paint, more masking and more patience.
  • Short-run kits can look excellent, but usually need test-fitting and filler.

Aftermarket priorities

Spend where it shows. Cockpit belts, wheels, exhausts and decals usually produce a better return than hidden internal detail.

  • Open canopy: cockpit matters.
  • Closed canopy: belts and instrument panel are usually enough.
  • Weathered aircraft: decals and paint accuracy matter more than invisible resin.

Market checks

Before buying, check the kit variant against the aircraft guide: engine, canopy, wing type, gun fit, propeller, tropical filter, radar, ordnance and markings.
