1940 collection guide

Best Battle of Britain Model Kits

Summer 1940 is the most popular period in British aircraft modelling. These are the kits to build a Battle of Britain collection, from the two famous RAF fighters to the Luftwaffe bombers they fought.

RAF fighters

Hawker Hurricane Mk.I

Arma Hobby’s Hurricane Mk.I (1/72 and 1/48) is the best current kit; Airfix’s modern-tool Hurricane is the value choice. See best Hurricane kit.

Supermarine Spitfire Mk.I

Tamiya’s 1/48 Spitfire Mk.I is the easiest accurate option; Airfix’s newer Mk.I kits are good value in both scales. See best Spitfire kit.

Boulton Paul Defiant and Bristol Blenheim

Worth adding for a fuller picture of RAF Fighter Command in 1940. Airfix has produced modern-tool kits of both in 1/72.

Luftwaffe aircraft

Messerschmitt Bf 109E

Eduard’s Bf 109E in 1/48 and 1/72 is the most detailed; Airfix’s newer 1/72 Bf 109E is a simple, cheap alternative.

Messerschmitt Bf 110C/D

Eduard’s 1/48 Bf 110 is the standard modern kit of the twin-engined Zerstörer.

Junkers Ju 87B Stuka

Airfix’s modern-tool Ju 87B (1/72 and 1/48) is an excellent, affordable Stuka.

Bombers: Do 17Z, He 111, Ju 88A

Airfix’s 1/72 Dornier Do 17Z is a fine modern kit. ICM’s 1/48 He 111H and Ju 88A families are detailed large bomber kits, best for builders with some experience.

Choosing a scale for a collection

For a collection that includes bombers, 1/72 is the practical choice: a 1/48 He 111 has a wingspan of almost 47 cm. If you only want the fighters, 1/48 gives more cockpit and weathering detail. Whatever you choose, keep the whole collection in one scale so the aircraft sit together properly on a shelf.

Painting for 1940

RAF fighters: Dark Earth and Dark Green upper surfaces; undersides changed through the summer from black and white to Sky. Luftwaffe fighters: RLM 02/71 or 74/75 uppers, often with mottled fuselage sides, over RLM 65; bombers in RLM 70/71 over 65. Squadron codes and yellow tactical paint on 109s changed during the battle, so date your subject carefully. Read Battle of Britain paint colours.

Quick answers

What scale is best for a Battle of Britain collection?

1/72, because it keeps the bombers to a manageable size. Use 1/48 if you only plan to build fighters.

What are the essential Battle of Britain kits?

Hurricane Mk.I, Spitfire Mk.I, Bf 109E, Bf 110, Ju 87B, Do 17Z, He 111 and Ju 88A.

What colour were RAF undersides in 1940?

Black and white early in the summer, then Sky from around June 1940, with variations between units.
