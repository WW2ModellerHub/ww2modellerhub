Quick answers

What colours do I need for a Bf 109?

Depending on variant and date, common colours include RLM 02, 65, 66, 70, 71, 74, 75, 76 and RLM 04 for theatre markings.

What is the hardest Luftwaffe technique?

Mottling is usually the hardest because it needs thin paint, low pressure and careful scale control.
