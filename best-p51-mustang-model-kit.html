Mustang kit buyer’s guide

Best P-51 Mustang Model Kit

The P-51D is one of the most popular model subjects in the world. The kits are good; the real challenge is the natural-metal finish and choosing between the razorback P-51B/C and the bubble-canopy P-51D.

P-51B/C or P-51D?

The P-51B/C has the original high-backed fuselage and the framed “birdcage” canopy (often replaced by the bulged Malcolm hood). The P-51D has the cut-down rear fuselage and bubble canopy, and is the version most people picture. RAF Mustang Mk.IIIs are the British equivalent of the P-51B/C.

Recommended Mustang kits

Best detail 1/48: Eduard P-51D

Eduard’s 1/48 P-51D is highly detailed and accurate, with variant parts for different production blocks. It is the best choice for an experienced builder.

Easiest 1/48: Tamiya P-51D

Tamiya’s 1/48 P-51D is an older kit but still builds quickly and cleanly. It is an excellent beginner Mustang.

Value 1/48 and 1/72: Airfix

Airfix’s modern-tool P-51D kits are inexpensive and build well. A good choice if you want a Mustang without spending much.

Showcase 1/32: Tamiya P-51D

Tamiya’s 1/32 P-51D/K is one of the most celebrated large-scale kits ever produced, with superb engineering and engine detail.

Natural-metal finish tips

Most late-war P-51Ds were unpainted aluminium, which is unforgiving. Sand seams carefully, polish the plastic, prime with gloss black, then use a lacquer metallic such as Alclad II, AK Xtreme Metal or Mr. Color Super Metallic. Vary the panels slightly for realism. Full method: natural metal finish guide.

Choosing a scale

Which scale?

  • 1/72 — small, cheap and quick. Ideal if you want a collection or have limited display space, but fine detail and painting are harder.
  • 1/48 — the most popular aircraft scale. Big enough for a detailed cockpit and visible weathering, small enough to finish in a few weeks.
  • 1/32 — showcase scale. Superb detail but expensive, large, and every flaw shows.

Quick answers

What is the best P-51D kit?

Eduard’s 1/48 P-51D for detail, Tamiya’s 1/48 P-51D for ease, and Tamiya’s 1/32 P-51D/K as a showcase build.

Were P-51Ds painted?

Most were natural metal, but some had olive drab upper surfaces, especially in 1944, and many had painted anti-glare panels and group markings.

What is the hardest part of building a Mustang?

The natural-metal finish, because it shows every seam and scratch.

What is the difference between a P-51B and P-51D?

The P-51B has a high rear fuselage and framed canopy; the P-51D has a cut-down rear fuselage and bubble canopy.
