Quick answers

What is the best first weathering technique?

A panel line wash and restrained exhaust staining are usually the best first techniques because they improve realism without overwhelming the model.

What weathering should I avoid?

Avoid random chipping, pure black panel lines and exhaust stains that ignore airflow or engine type.
