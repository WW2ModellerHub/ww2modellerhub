Choosing your Ki-43 Oscar kit

The kit choice starts with the correct variant. Use early Ki-43B/C kits for Japanese / IJAAF Ki-43 Oscars, Ki-43E/K kits for desert and Pacific subjects, and Ki-43N kits for late-war Allied operators. Choose by theatre, markings and decal sheet.

Aftermarket for the Ki-43 Oscar

Aftermarket is now split by purpose: decals and markings, Pacific/VLR loadout, cockpit, exterior detail and masks. For Pacific subjects, drop tanks, sun-faded weathering and correct unit markings are as important as cockpit detail.

Japanese / IJAAF Ki-43 Oscar paint colours

Ki-43 Oscar colour treatment is dominated by two main looks: early-war Olive Drab over Neutral Gray on Ki-43B/C aircraft, and the classic late-war natural-metal finish on Ki-43E/N aircraft. Theatre colours, anti-glare panels, invasion stripes and tail markings do most of the visual work on a finished model.

Roundel & marking notes

National insignia: Standard Japanese / IJAAF star-and-bar markings carried on wings and fuselage, with blue outline and red-bar period changes depending on date.

Theatre colours: Nose colours, tail markings, checker patterns and squadron codes vary sharply by unit — one of the reasons the Ki-43 Oscar is such a strong decal subject.

Common extras: Olive Drab anti-glare panel, black serials, invasion stripes in mid-1944, and highly visible unit colours on spinner, nose or tail depending on subject.
