How to use this on a build

1. Pick aircraft and theatre

A Spitfire in 1940, a Malta Spitfire, and a late-war 2nd TAF aircraft need different paint logic.

2. Choose one paint brand

Do not mix brands blindly. Each range dries differently and shifts under varnish.

3. Scale the colour

For 1/72, slightly lighten dark colours. For 1/48 and 1/32, preserve more contrast.

4. Weather after markings

Dust, exhaust, fading and chipping should match the aircraft’s theatre and operating surface.
