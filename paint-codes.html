P-40 Warhawk / Kittyhawk paint routes

For P-40 models, choose the colour system by theatre: AVG Flying Tigers and RAF Tomahawks usually use RAF-style Dark Earth / Dark Green with light undersides; Desert Air Force Kittyhawks use Middle Stone / Dark Earth over Azure Blue; USAAF Pacific Warhawks use Olive Drab 41 over Neutral Gray 43; late P-40N subjects may show heavily faded Olive Drab, replacement panels and local theatre markings.
