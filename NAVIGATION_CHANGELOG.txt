Navigation update
=================

Changed the site-wide Aircraft dropdown into grouped sections:
- Allied fighters
- Allied bombers
- Axis fighters
- Axis bombers / attack
- Specialist aircraft
- Research tools

Live/current pages remain real links. Future aircraft such as B-17, B-25, P-47, P-38, Hawker Typhoon, Ju 87 and others are shown as Planned but are deliberately not clickable yet, avoiding broken links until their pages exist.

Also clarified that Hawker Typhoon means the WW2 RAF fighter-bomber, not the Eurofighter Typhoon.

Technical fixes included:
- Solid, non-transparent dropdown background.
- Wider desktop grouped dropdown.
- Mobile/Safari safe fixed dropdown panel.
- Click-outside and Escape-to-close behaviour.
- ARIA expanded state on the Aircraft button.
