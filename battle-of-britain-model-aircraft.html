Quick answers

Which aircraft should I build first for a Battle of Britain shelf?

Start with a Hurricane or Spitfire, then add a Bf 109E. Add larger bombers later once you are comfortable with masking and bigger builds.

What are the key Battle of Britain paint colours?

RAF builds often use Dark Green, Dark Earth and Sky Type S. Luftwaffe aircraft commonly use RLM 70/71/65 or variant-specific RLM combinations.
