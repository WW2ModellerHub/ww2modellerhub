Choosing your He 111 kit

Nine injection-moulded He 111 kits are currently available across three scales. For a Battle of Britain build in 1:48, the Airfix A05127A is the recommendation for most builders — excellent accuracy, great fit, and outstanding value. If you want the finest He 111 kit ever made, the Arma Hobby AH40015 Expert Set is extraordinary. In 1:72, the Arma Hobby 70019 / AH70024 is in a different league. Aftermarket for the He 111

The Airfix kit cockpit benefits hugely from the CMK CMK4352 resin set — it's the single best upgrade for the money. For gun barrels, the Master Model AM-48-172 brass set makes an immediate visible difference. Eduard's pre-cut canopy masks save time and improve results over freehand. Photo-etch is optional on the Airfix kit but nearly mandatory on the older Hasegawa and Eduard plastics. Luftwaffe RLM colour guide

Every He 111 in the Battle of Britain wore the same three-colour MAP scheme: Dark Earth and Dark Green on the upper surfaces in a disruptive splinter pattern, and Sky (Type S) on the undersides, spinner and fuselage band. The table below gives matched codes across all four major paint brands. Humbrol 29/30/90 are the classic references; Tamiya XF-52/XF-61/XF-21 are good for airbrush users; Vallejo offers superior coverage for brush painters. The cockpit, wheel bays and gear legs were Interior Green (Humbrol 78).

Roundel & marking notes

July–Aug 1940: Type A1 roundels (red/white/blue, no yellow ring) on fuselage. Type A upper wings. Type B lower wings (red/blue only). Large fin flash.

Sep–Oct 1940: Yellow outer ring on fuselage roundels increasingly common. Yellow border on upper wing roundels appearing late in period.

Code letters: Sky BS381C-216. Approx 24 inch height. Serial in black on rear fuselage port side only.
