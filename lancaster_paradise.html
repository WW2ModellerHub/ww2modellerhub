Choosing your Lancaster kit

Choose the variant first. A standard B.I/B.III, Dambuster B.III Special, Tallboy aircraft and Grand Slam aircraft are not interchangeable. Use stable manufacturer/category links where possible and confirm scale, boxing and parts before buying decals.

Aftermarket for the Lancaster

Masks are the first upgrade because the Lancaster is dominated by canopy and turret glazing. Brass barrels, wheels and selected photo-etch are useful, but only if matched to your exact kit and scale.

Lancaster paint colours

The classic wartime finish is Dark Green/Dark Earth over Night. The modelling challenge is scale black: do not leave the underside as a single flat pure black.
