Build guide
Lancasters are not just big Spitfires. The modelling job is about scale, surface finish, nacelle staining, turret clarity, bomb-bay choice and not mixing variants.
Stage 1Choose the exact aircraft first

Pick squadron, serial, code and date before buying decals or choosing a bomb load. Dambuster, Tallboy, Grand Slam and standard bomb-bay Lancasters are not interchangeable.

Stage 2Interior restraint

Most cockpit and crew-station detail becomes shadowed. Focus on the cockpit, turrets, bomb aimer's blister and open hatches if displayed.

Stage 3Nacelles and exhaust staining

The Merlin exhaust streaks across the nacelles and upper wings are the visual signature of a hard-worked Lancaster. Build them in thin brown-grey-black layers.

Stage 4Final finish

Upper surfaces are RAF Dark Green and Dark Earth; undersides Night. Keep the finish operational and matt, not glossy museum black.

Paint and weathering notes
Upper camouflageDark Green / Dark Earth

Use RAF MAP colours. Slight fading and panel variation is useful, but avoid USAAF-style heavy chipping.

UndersidesNight black

Use a broken black: black plus dark grey/brown modulation. Add crew access scuffs, bomb-bay grime and hydraulic staining.

TurretsClear parts matter

Mask carefully. Turret framing, clear glazing and gun barrels are often what make or break a Lancaster build.
