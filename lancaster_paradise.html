Choosing your Lancaster kit

The Lancaster build starts with variant choice. A standard B.I/B.III, Dambuster B.III Special, Tallboy aircraft and Grand Slam aircraft need different parts and references. Do not pick decals until you have chosen squadron, serial, code and date.

Aftermarket for the Lancaster

Masks are essential because the Lancaster is dominated by canopies and turrets. Brass gun barrels, wheels, seatbelts and selected photo-etch can lift the model, but check scale and kit manufacturer carefully.
