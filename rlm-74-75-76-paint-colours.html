Luftwaffe fighter colours

RLM 74, 75 and 76 Paint Colours

RLM 74, 75 and 76 were the standard Luftwaffe day-fighter colours from 1941 until late in the war. If you build a Bf 109G or Fw 190A, these are the paints you need.

What the colours are

  • RLM 74 Graugrün — a dark grey-green, the darker of the two upper colours.
  • RLM 75 Grauviolett — a mid grey with a slight violet tint.
  • RLM 76 Lichtblau — a very pale blue-grey for undersides and fuselage sides. Its shade varied noticeably, especially late in the war.

How they were applied

The wings and tailplanes carried a hard-edged splinter pattern of 74 and 75. The fuselage spine was 74/75, blending into RLM 76 sides, which were then mottled with 74, 75 and sometimes 02 or 70. Mottling varied hugely between factories and units: some aircraft had light, sparse mottles, others almost solid coverage.

From 1944, RLM 81 Braunviolett and 82 Hellgrün began replacing 74 and 75, and many aircraft mixed old and new colours.

Painting tips

  1. Spray RLM 76 first on undersides and sides.
  2. Mask and spray the 75 then 74 splinter on the wings.
  3. Mottle the fuselage sides at low pressure (10–15 psi) with well-thinned paint, close to the surface.
  4. Overspray very thin 76 to soften mottles that are too stark.

Quick answers

When were RLM 74/75/76 used?

From around late 1941 until the end of the war, increasingly alongside RLM 81/82/83 from 1944.

What is the Mr Hobby code for RLM 74?

H68 (RLM 75 is H69 and RLM 76 is H417).

Which is darker, RLM 74 or 75?

RLM 74 Graugrün is the darker colour.
