Soviet assault aviation campaign · 1941–1945

The Il-2 Sturmovik became one of the defining Soviet ground-attack aircraft of the Eastern Front. For modellers, the Il-2 is about its armoured cockpit, broad wing, rockets and bombs, worn AMT camouflage, winter whitewash and muddy frontline airstrips. This page focuses on model-friendly Il-2 regiments, key pilots, camouflage and markings. Click any card to see pilots, markings, bases and decal-ready subject ideas.

Choosing your Il-2 kit

The kit choice now needs a theatre-first approach: early single-seat 1941–42 Il-2s, two-seat Il-2M machines from Stalingrad onward, or late-war arrow-wing / Type 3 aircraft. Pick your subject by theatre, winter whitewash, rockets/bombs, rear gunner fit and Guards unit markings.

Aftermarket for the Il-2 Sturmovik

Aftermarket is split by purpose: decals and markings, cockpit/interior, armour and exterior detail, rockets / RS-82 stores and masks. For Il-2 subjects, correct wing type, gun fairings, rear gunner fit and weathered Eastern Front markings matter as much as cockpit detail.

Il-2 Sturmovik paint colours

Il-2 Sturmovik colour treatment changes by period: early-war AII Green / AII Blue, the classic mid-war AMT-4 Green / AMT-6 Black over AMT-7 Blue, and some later AMT-1 / AMT-4 / dark grey combinations on late-war aircraft. Tactical numbers, Guards badges, patriotic slogans and winter whitewash do much of the visual work on a finished model.

Roundel & marking notes

National insignia: Standard VVS star-and-bar markings carried on wings and fuselage, with blue outline and red-bar period changes depending on date.

Unit colours: Tactical numbers, Guards badges, patriotic slogans and star outlines vary between units — one of the reasons the Il-2 Sturmovik is such a strong decal subject.

Common extras: RS-82 / RS-132 rockets, bombs, winter distemper, tactical slogans and field-applied repairs are all strong finishing choices depending on the subject.
