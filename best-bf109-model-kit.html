Bf 109 kit buyer’s guide

Best Messerschmitt Bf 109 Model Kit

Like the Spitfire, the Bf 109 changed shape through the war. Choose the version first — the angular “Emil”, the rounded “Friedrich”, the “Gustav” or the late “K” — then pick the best kit of it.

Know your 109

  • Bf 109E “Emil” — 1939–41, Battle of Britain and early desert. Squared wingtips, braced tailplane, RLM 02/71 over 65 or 74/75/76 schemes.
  • Bf 109F “Friedrich” — 1941–42, rounded wingtips, cleaner nose, unbraced tail. Classic Eastern Front and North Africa subject.
  • Bf 109G “Gustav” — 1942–45, the most produced version, with many sub-types: early G-2s, the bulged G-6, “Erla” canopies, tall tails and late G-14/G-10s.
  • Bf 109K — late-war, often in the later RLM 81/82/83 colours.

See Emil vs Friedrich for the visual differences.

Recommended Bf 109 kits

Best detail 1/48: Eduard Bf 109E, F and G

Eduard has tooled modern 109s across the E, F and G families in 1/48. They are detailed and accurate, with many sub-variant options and good decal sheets. They need care with parts selection because one sprue set covers several versions.

Easiest 1/48 Gustav: Tamiya Bf 109G-6

Tamiya’s 1/48 Bf 109G-6 is a straightforward, well-fitting kit that suits less experienced builders who want a good-looking late-war 109.

Best 1/72

Eduard’s 1/72 Bf 109 kits give the most detail; Airfix’s newer-tool 1/72 Bf 109E is a simple, inexpensive Battle of Britain option. Fine Molds also produces very fine small-scale 109s.

Choosing a scale

Which scale?

  • 1/72 — small, cheap and quick. Ideal if you want a collection or have limited display space, but fine detail and painting are harder.
  • 1/48 — the most popular aircraft scale. Big enough for a detailed cockpit and visible weathering, small enough to finish in a few weeks.
  • 1/32 — showcase scale. Superb detail but expensive, large, and every flaw shows.

Painting a Bf 109

Luftwaffe colours are identified by RLM numbers. A Battle of Britain 109E typically wears RLM 02/71 or 74/75 splinter on the wings with mottled fuselage sides over RLM 65 or 76; Gustavs use RLM 74/75/76 with soft mottling; late 109s often have RLM 81/82 uppers. Get the mottle right and the model looks right — see the mottling technique guide and RLM 74/75/76.

Quick answers

What is the best Bf 109 kit?

For 1/48, Eduard’s Bf 109E, F and G kits are the most detailed; Tamiya’s Bf 109G-6 is the easiest good Gustav.

Which Bf 109 is easiest to paint?

A Battle of Britain Bf 109E or an early Bf 109F, because the mottling is simpler than on many late-war Gustavs.

What are Bf 109 colours?

Typically RLM 74 and 75 on the upper surfaces with RLM 76 below for most of the war, and RLM 02/71 over RLM 65 earlier.

What is the difference between a Bf 109E and F?

The F has rounded wingtips, a cleaner nose and spinner, and an unbraced tailplane.
