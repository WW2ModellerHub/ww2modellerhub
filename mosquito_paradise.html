de Havilland Mosquito.

Built of wood and faster than most fighters of its era — the de Havilland Mosquito is one of the most technically interesting builds in WW2 modelling, with a unique wood-grain airframe texture and a huge range of roles and markings to choose from.

A reference framework for Mosquito modellers covering the birch ply airframe finish, night fighter and fighter-bomber colour schemes, kit options across scales and markings for the main variants.

Modeller quick start

Airfix's 1:72 Mosquito FB.VI is the accessible entry point. For 1:48, Tamiya's tooling remains a benchmark for fit and detail. The key modelling challenge on any Mosquito is replicating the smooth, painted-wood surface texture — subtle streaking and minimal chipping is the correct approach.
