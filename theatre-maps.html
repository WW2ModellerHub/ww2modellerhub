Theatre summaries

Quick reference cards for what each theatre is most useful for on a World War II aircraft modelling reference site.

Battle of Britain

Best for Hurricane and Spitfire squadron markings, sector stations, RAF camouflage and early-war weathering.

Linked aircraft: Hurricane · Spitfire

8th Air Force

Core board for B-17 Bomb Group and P-51 Fighter Group subjects in East Anglia, including famous station identities and nose/tail colour cues.

Linked aircraft: B-17 · P-51

Mediterranean & Italy

Useful for Red Tails, desert-to-Italian transition subjects, bomber support and faded, dusty finishes.

Linked aircraft: P-51 · B-17 · future RAF / Regia Aeronautica

Pacific & Rabaul

Zero, late-war Japanese aircraft and US long-range operations need tropical fading, coral dust and carrier or island-base context.

Linked aircraft: A6M Zero · future Corsair / Hellcat / Wildcat

Eastern Front

The right home for Fw 190, future Bf 109, Il-2, Yak and La fighters with harder weathering, field strips and AMT / RLM colour context.

Linked aircraft: Fw 190 · future Soviet aircraft
