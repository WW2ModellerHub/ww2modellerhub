Ratings and difficulty methodology

How Ratings Are Decided

How WW2 Modeller decides kit ratings, aircraft difficulty scores and modelling complexity labels.

Kit ratings

Kit ratings are practical modeller ratings, not absolute engineering scores. They balance fit, accuracy, surface detail, cockpit detail, decals, availability, price/value, aftermarket need and beginner friendliness.

A kit with perfect detail but difficult fit may score lower for beginners than a slightly simpler kit that builds cleanly.

Aircraft difficulty scores

Aircraft difficulty is judged from kit availability, camouflage complexity, canopy masking, decal complexity, aftermarket need, weathering difficulty and size/display difficulty.

Difficulty labels are intended as build-planning guidance: Beginner, Medium, Advanced or Expert. They are not a judgement on whether a subject is worth building.
