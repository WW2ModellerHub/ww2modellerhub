Mediterranean / North Africa Route

A theatre route for desert light, Malta pressure, dusty forward strips, tropical filters, sun-faded camouflage and mixed Allied, Luftwaffe and Italian subjects.

The story

The Mediterranean is not one single look. It moves from Malta and convoy defence to North African desert fighting, Sicily, Italy and the Balkans. Aircraft operated in fierce sun, abrasive dust, salt air and improvised conditions. This is where a model can tell a strong visual story through faded paint, theatre bands, sand filters and rougher field finishes.

Aircraft to build

Good starting subjects are Spitfire V, P-40, Bf 109 Trop, Macchi C.202/C.205, Ju 87, Beaufighter and Wellington-style support aircraft. Italian fighters are especially useful here because they add a completely different visual language from RAF and Luftwaffe schemes.

Paint logic

Think warm, faded and sun-beaten. RAF desert schemes bring Middle Stone, Dark Earth and Azure Blue into play. Luftwaffe tropical aircraft use RLM 78, 79 and 80 variations. Italian aircraft can involve sand, green smoke-ring/mottle finishes and theatre bands. Malta aircraft may be more complex, with overpainting and disputed blue-grey appearances depending on subject.

Weathering logic

Dust should sit around tyres, lower surfaces, wing roots, filters and maintenance areas. Sun fading should break up upper surfaces without making everything chalk white. Salt and heat can make markings and paint look tired. Use restrained chipping on leading edges, access panels and walkways.

Good modelling route

Build a P-40 or Spitfire V for the Allied desert route, then a Bf 109 Trop or Macchi for contrast. This theatre rewards research because a small change in place and date can completely change the colour story.
