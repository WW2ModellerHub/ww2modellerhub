Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026. This policy explains what information WW2 Modeller Hub collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Who we are

WW2 Modeller Hub is a scale modelling reference website covering WW2 aircraft. We are based in the United Kingdom. If you have questions about this policy, contact us via the About page.

What information we collect

We collect minimal data. Specifically:

  • Cookies: We use browser localStorage (a type of cookie storage) to remember your cookie consent preference. No personal data is stored.
  • Advertising cookies: If Google AdSense is enabled, Google may place advertising cookies on your device to show relevant ads. These are third-party cookies controlled by Google, not by us.
  • Analytics: We may use basic analytics to understand how many people visit the site. This data is anonymised and not linked to individuals.
  • No registration: We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use this site.

Google AdSense and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit here and/or prior visits to other websites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links to retailers including Hannants and Amazon UK. If you click these links and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission at no cost to you. This helps fund the running of the site. Affiliate links are clearly marked where relevant.

External links

This site links to third-party websites including YouTube, the Imperial War Museum, Hannants, Amazon and manufacturer websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have rights regarding any personal data we hold about you. As we collect no personal data directly, there is very little data to manage. For any questions, please contact us via the About page.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Cookie consent

When you first visit the site, a banner asks for your consent to cookies. Clicking "Accept" stores a cookie in your browser's localStorage to remember your preference. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.
